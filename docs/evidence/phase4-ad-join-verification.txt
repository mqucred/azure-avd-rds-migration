--- Note ---
sAMAccountName is truncated to 15 characters by AD's own limit -- this is expected,
not an error. The full storage account name is preserved in the CN/Name attribute
and in the SPN, which is what Kerberos auth actually relies on. Always query AD
computer objects for storage accounts by Name, not by the (possibly truncated)
sAMAccountName -- Get-ADComputer -Identity "<full storage account name>" will fail
with ADIdentityNotFoundException if the name exceeds 15 characters.
